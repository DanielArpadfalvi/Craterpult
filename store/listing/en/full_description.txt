Lob. Blast. Outlast.

Craterpult is a turn-based artillery game made for one thumb. Lead a crew of little neon creatures across islands you can blow apart: drag back to aim, let go to fire, and watch the ground crumble into brand-new craters. Every shot reshapes the battlefield, so the hill that sheltered you this turn may be gone by the next.

No ads. No energy timers. No loot boxes. Just a complete game you can play anywhere, even offline.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Take turns: walk, jump or backflip into position, then use one weapon
• Slingshot aiming: drag back from your crater and release, with a dotted flight-path preview
• Fully destructible terrain: blast holes, drill tunnels, bridge gaps with girders
• Watch the wind: it changes every turn and pushes your shots around
• Crates drop health and extra weapons, and in sudden death the water starts to rise
• Knock the other crews out of the fight or into the sea. Last crew standing wins

16 WEAPONS
Bazooka, grenade with an adjustable fuse, shotgun, cluster bomb, mortar, napalm, a self-steering seeker, air strike, dynamite, mines, a wall-hopping crawler bomb, power punch, drill, girder, teleport, and an earthquake that rattles the whole island.

WAYS TO PLAY
• CAMPAIGN – 30 hand-tuned missions in three chapters, each with three stars to earn: win fast, lose nobody, or win with a single weapon
• QUICK MATCH – a freshly generated map every time, against bots on five levels from Rookie to Ace
• PASS & PLAY – two players, one phone, with a cover screen between turns so nobody peeks
• DAILY CHALLENGE – a new battle with a new twist every day: low gravity, hurricane winds, grenades only and more. Keep your streak going and beat your best score
• Five map styles: Hills, Islands, Cavern, Towers and Flats

BOTS THAT REALLY PLAY
The bots use the same physics you do and try out their shots before they fire. Rookies miss in style; Aces read the wind and rarely give you a second chance.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Name your team and pick its colour; with the Full Version, campaign stars also unlock bonus hats (crown, horns, halo)
• Stats for wins, knockouts, accuracy, biggest shot and favourite weapon
• Adjustable turn time and aim-preview length
• Colour-blind friendly: teams can wear distinct shapes, not just colours
• Reduced motion, larger text, sound and haptics options
• Crisp neon vector visuals, punchy explosions and screen shake
• English and Hungarian

FAIR, ONE-TIME UNLOCK
Craterpult is free to download, and the free version is a complete game: the whole first campaign chapter (10 missions), quick matches against Rookie and Easy bots with teams of up to three on the Hills and Islands maps, and pass & play with no limits.

One optional purchase, the Full Version, unlocks everything else for good: chapters 2 and 3 (20 more missions), bot levels 3 to 5, four-strong teams, the Daily Challenge, the Cavern, Towers and Flats maps, and the cosmetic hats you earn with campaign stars. No subscriptions, no ads, no energy, nothing to grind for with real money. Already bought it? Tap Restore purchases in Settings or on the Full Version screen.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
No account, no tracking, no analytics. Your progress is saved on your device, and every mode plays fully offline.
